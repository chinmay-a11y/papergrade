Why not a generic LLM? Indic handwriting + in-language voice is precisely where sovereign Indian models win — and where a chatbot wrapper can't follow.
Technical depth · beyond API stitching

The system knows what it doesn't know — and proves it.

Confidence is measured, not self-reported.
Each crop is read twice (one pass rotated + rescaled); the edit distance between the two transcripts is the uncertainty. Disagreement, rendered:
जल का क्वथनांक कवथनाक सौ डिग्री सेल्सियस होता है।
Below threshold → routed to a human. A model that abstains demos better than one that always answers.
  • Per-question segmentation — never a whole page for one grade
  • Rubric → JSON, cached — grading is constrained, auditable output
  • Forced abstention — needs_human: true instead of a guess

Business impact & moat · path to customers

A wedge into every regional-language classroom in India.

Market. State boards, coaching chains, ed-tech platforms.
Formative assessment is the wedge; board-exam evaluation is the TAM — across 1.47M schools · 248M students · 22 languages. Coaching chains sell to parents, and a parent who hears why in their own language is a renewal.
