What you get

Grading you can defend to a board.

✍️

Per-question grading

Each answer is cropped and graded against its own rubric line — never a whole page for one grade. Partial marks, per criterion.

📊

Measured confidence

Every answer is read twice; the disagreement between passes is the confidence — a real measurement, not a model self-report. Low confidence routes to you.

🔊

Feedback parents hear

Bulbul speaks each student's feedback in Hindi, delivered by a QR stamped on the paper — plus two practice questions. No student login.

🎯

Concept analytics

“5 of 6 students missed friction.” One query surfaces the misconception the whole class shares — the artefact a principal actually buys.

⚖️

Agreement, honestly

Quadratic weighted kappa vs. teacher marks — reported with sample size. QWK 0.96 (n=20) on the demo set.

🇮🇳

Data stays in India

Sovereign Indian models on Indian infrastructure. Student names are stripped before any model call; every score change is audit-logged.
